Canopy dashboard system

A calm, dense system for data-heavy screens: flat surfaces, hairline borders, 13px type and one hot accent. This page draws every component with css/app.css, the same file the app loads. Rules and reasons are in docs/design-system.md.

Step rail and Back / Next

For a flow with ordered steps (Create pilot plan): its own page with a rail of steps you cannot click, so no step is skipped. The step you are on is orange, finished steps are ticked, and Back and Next stay at the foot. On a phone the rail becomes a thin progress bar. Nothing on a step page is folded away.

App shell

A sidebar that only takes you to pages (grouped Explore, Pilot, About, with the data status card at the foot), a top bar with the breadcrumb and the tools for the page you are on (tour, share, export), then the page.
