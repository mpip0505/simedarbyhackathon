City of Elmina · example data

Where should trees go first?

This tool ranks sites in City of Elmina by how much a new tree could help: cooling hot, bare ground, linking green areas and shading footpaths. You decide which sites to plant. The tool never decides for you.

  1. 1

    See where to plant first

    The map shows every candidate site, ranked. Move the three sliders to say what matters most to you, and the ranking follows.

  2. 2

    Check why a site ranks high

    Click any site to see its score, what drives it and what the data cannot tell you. The Sites page lists all of them side by side.

  3. 3

    Track your sites

    Add the sites you like to your shortlist, then follow each one in the Tracker: visit it, decide, plant, and check the trees. A person still has to check each site on the ground before anything is planted.

Good to know: the sites are examples (simulated), and scores are previews until the scoring model is connected. This is not a tree count or a carbon figure.

        Limits of this data

          What we checked, and what we did not

          • Checked: there were enough clear satellite images through cloud; the result depends on the greenness cut-off; two other satellite maps disagree on tree cover; the top of the list holds up under 2,000 plausible weightings.
          • Not done: no field plots, tree inventory or drone survey, so nothing here shows the ranking is right. A field check sheet is ready for the first walk.

          Add a city

          Canopia has real data for one township today: City of Elmina. The method has not been run on any other township, so there is nothing to switch to yet.

          What a second city needs, in short:

          • Its study area and boundary as settings, instead of the Elmina box built into the data pipeline.
          • A site-ID prefix for the township, and its own cross-check numbers and caveat wording.
          • Its corridor outline, if the developer has one (otherwise an approximate one is drawn from OpenStreetMap parks).
          • Its own cached data behind its own API. Add it to js/projects.js and it appears in this menu.

          Our estimate, not measured: one to two days of engineering for the first extra township. Details are in docs/commercial-case.md, "What repeating it for another township needs".
