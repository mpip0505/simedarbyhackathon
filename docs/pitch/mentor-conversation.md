# Mentor conversation: email, questions and answer sheet

Written for: the team member who contacts a Sime Darby Property mentor. **No conversation has happened yet, so there are no answers in this file, and none should be invented.** The three questions are the open ones in [commercial-case.md](../commercial-case.md).

## The email (about 90 words)
> Subject: 15 minutes for Team Triple T (Planet track)
>
> Hi [Name], we're Team Triple T. We built a tree-planting prioritiser, piloted on City of Elmina with simulated sites because we don't have your list. Could we have 15 minutes this week? Three questions: who owns the planting budget decision, how planting sites are chosen today, and what evidence your sustainability reporting needs. A one-line answer to each would shape our pitch. Demo and code: [link]. Thank you.

Send it with the live link or the video if you have one. Do not attach claims the docs do not support.

## The three questions (ask in this order, then stop)
1. **Who owns the planting decision and its budget?** (a role is enough; it tells us who the user and the buyer are)
2. **How are planting sites chosen today?** (what data, what meeting, what is hardest to justify)
3. **What evidence would a sustainability report need from a tool like this?** (what would they quote, and what would they refuse to)

Optional, if time remains: *What would make you distrust a ranking like this?* and *What would a first pilot with you look like?*

## Rules for using the answers
- Quote nothing as Sime Darby Property's view without the person's permission. Write "one SDP mentor said" only if they agree.
- One conversation is one data point. Say "one mentor conversation", never "stakeholder validation".
- Change a claim in the deck or docs only if an answer contradicts it, and say what changed.
